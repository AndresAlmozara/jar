# JAR Evolution

This is the concise chronological account. Current architecture is in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md); exact scientific results are in
[RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

## H0 — owned baseline

H0 established the owned runtime, routing modules, safety gates, and product
benchmark infrastructure. It gave later experiments a stable system to change
and a controlled baseline to compare against. Its detailed archive anchor is
retained in the private development repository.

## Lean V1 — canonical product

The ALL-IN treatment added lean packed TypeSafe decisions and operational
coverage protection without replacing the older M5 or deterministic M6. That
combination improved the routing surface without surrendering deterministic
authority, so it became JAR V1. The detailed daily-driver checkpoint remains in
the private development archive.

## TypeSafe/JEV research

The project froze a vendor corpus, audited primitive and request semantics,
mapped JAR, verified design propositions against sources, and reconciled the
work with an independent routing audit. This established the boundary used by
the product: semantic judgment may propose relevance, ranking, or
classification, while identity, eligibility, policy, materialization, and
verification remain deterministic. Final findings and treatment dispositions
are retained in the public [research ledger](RESEARCH.md); the raw provenance
and copied vendor corpus remain private and are not distributed.

## Monolithic V2

V2 improved some local mechanisms and SOFT efficiency, preserved M7, regressed
M5, and imposed severe HARD overhead. It was scientifically useful but not
promoted. Local mechanism gains did not add up to a better product. That result
changed the next question from “should V2 replace V1?” to “can any one component
beat its V1 counterpart?”

## Isolated component program

Research returned to V1 and introduced explicit single-component treatments.
M6 recall-first and operational M8 were tested separately. Reusable fixtures,
oracles, isolation checks, pair-identity controls, and telemetry became
permanent diagnostics; one-shot machinery and raw outputs remained in Git
history. This made each result attributable to one changed mechanism instead
of a monolithic treatment.

## M8 closure

The first M8 product matrix was rejected as causal-invalid because the arms did
not share pre-treatment identity. M8-v2 repaired that boundary. The corrected
campaign found mixed mechanism behavior, weaker aggregate product economics,
and a poor activation predictor. Operational M8 was not promoted and the M8
line closed. Its evaluator and causal regressions remain because a rejected
treatment can still leave valuable verification infrastructure.

## Final M6 closure

```text
isolated M6-r1 emerged as the strongest candidate
        ↓
confirmation reproduced a latency signal but mixed token behavior
        ↓
permanent diagnostics eliminated weaker variants
        ↓
current-JEV qualification validated r1 semantics
        ↓
targeted product testing exposed regime-dependent benefit
        ↓
r1 was not promoted as always-on M6
        ↓
JAR V1 remained canonical
        ↓
research paused and daily-use / maintenance mode began
```

M6-r1 remains the strongest non-promoted specialist, particularly under
selection pressure. Indirect relation/import and the larger non-exhaustive
repository path favored V1, so the aggregate result did not justify an
always-on architecture change. The mechanism was real; the general product case
was not.

## Frozen state

```text
research closure → JAR V1 canonical → passive always-on telemetry
                 → two-week ordinary-use observation phase
```

Always-on telemetry was added after the research freeze to observe ordinary
daily-driver behavior. It is a passive product capability, not a reopened
campaign and not a V1-versus-r1 comparison.

The final tree keeps JAR V1 as the sole default, permanent evaluators, compact
source provenance, and [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) as the single scientific
ledger. The private development archive owns retired campaign detail. No
component research program or automatic follow-up experiment is active.
